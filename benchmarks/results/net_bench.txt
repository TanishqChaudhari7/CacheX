  PING is the floor: a full round trip that touches no cache data,
  so the gap between it and SET/GET is the cache work, and PING
  itself is what the transport costs.

  Percentiles here are real measurements, not tick counts: a round
  trip is tens of microseconds against a 41 ns clock tick -- unlike the
  in-process benchmark, where the tick swamped the operation.
